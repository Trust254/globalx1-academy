Privacy Policy

Global-X Learning Academy (“Global-X”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects the privacy of learners and visitors. This policy explains what personal information may be collected when you use our website and learning services, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 14 November 2026

Please note: This policy describes the current academy website and services. It is not a statement that Global-X is a government body, university, regulator or accredited institution. Our course-completion certificates are academy-issued certificates.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Global-X Learning Academy operates this online learning platform. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at globalxlearningacademy@gmail.com. We have not designated a separate Data Protection Officer for this website; please use this contact address.

This policy does not state that the academy is registered as a company or data controller with any regulator. If a formal operating entity or privacy contact is appointed, this page will be updated with its verified details.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may process:

  • Account information: your name, email address, password credential (stored as a password hash rather than readable password), account role and account creation details.
  • Learning records: courses you enrol in, lesson completion, exam attempts and scores, progress, and certificates issued.
  • Certificate verification information: when a certificate is checked using its unique code, the verification page can display the learner name, course title, certificate code, issue date and issuer details.
  • Messages and enquiries: information you choose to send to us by email or through support features.
  • Technical information: information that hosting and security providers may record, such as IP address, browser/device details, request timestamps and error or security logs.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents or sensitive personal information through the academy help assistant or ordinary email unless we specifically request it through a suitable secure process.

3. How we use information

We use information only as reasonably needed to:

  • create and secure learner accounts and authenticate sign-ins;
  • provide course access, enrolment, lessons, assessments and learning progress;
  • calculate course completion and certificate eligibility, issue certificates and support certificate verification;
  • send service messages such as account registration and sign-in notifications when email delivery is configured;
  • respond to support requests, maintain the platform, prevent misuse and troubleshoot technical problems; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations and protect the rights and security of learners and the academy.

We do not sell learner personal information. We do not currently use the academy website to send advertising or sell marketing lists.

4. Automated tools and the academy help assistant

The Global-X Help Guide currently provides automated, predefined answers about academy navigation, courses, lessons, examinations and certificates. In the current website implementation, its answers are generated within the page from predefined rules; it is not a general-purpose AI model and the questions are not sent to an external AI provider by that feature.

We may introduce additional automated or AI-supported features in future. If a new feature sends personal information to an external AI provider or uses automated processing in a materially different way, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information into the help assistant.

5. Email and service providers

We use service providers to host and operate the platform and deliver particular features. Current services include:

  • GitHub Pages: hosting the public academy website.
  • Render: hosting the academy application programming interface and related server functions.
  • Resend: email delivery for account-related notifications when the sender domain and email service are correctly configured.
  • YouTube: video playback where a course lesson uses an embedded YouTube video. YouTube may collect information under its own privacy terms when its player is loaded.
  • QR code service: the certificate print view requests a QR image from an external QR code service. The QR image encodes the certificate verification address and code.

These providers may process technical or personal information as necessary to supply their services and under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not control their independent practices. Provider arrangements and services may change, and this list will be updated when material changes are made.

6. When information may be shared

We may share limited information with the service providers listed above to operate the academy, with authorised academy personnel who need it to support learning and administration, or where disclosure is required to comply with law, protect safety, investigate misuse or enforce our rights. We do not make learner records publicly available except for the limited certificate details returned when someone uses a valid certificate verification code.

7. International processing

Our hosting, email, video and other service providers may process information in countries outside the country where you live. Where this happens, we will take steps appropriate to the circumstances and applicable law to protect the information and use reputable providers. The exact processing location may depend on the provider's infrastructure and service configuration.

8. Cookies, browser storage and similar technologies

The current academy website uses browser local storage to keep a learner signed in between visits. This is not a browser cookie, but it stores an authentication token on your device. Signing out removes the stored token. You can clear site data in your browser, although doing so may sign you out.

We do not currently intentionally install advertising or analytics cookies in the academy website code. Embedded third-party content, including YouTube, may use its own cookies or similar technologies when loaded and is governed by the provider's settings and policies.

9. How long we keep information

We keep account and learning information for as long as it is needed to provide the learning service, maintain learner progress and verify certificates. We may retain limited records for longer where reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution, legal obligations or legitimate record-keeping. Technical logs and support correspondence are retained only for as long as needed for their operational purpose. We will delete or anonymise information when it is no longer required, subject to applicable obligations and technical backups.

10. Keeping information secure

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including password hashing, authenticated access to learner functions and restricted access to administrative functions. No website, internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a unique password, keep it confidential and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, or a copy of information. You may also be able to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

To make a request, email globalxlearningacademy@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and describe what you need. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity before acting. We aim to respond within the period required by applicable law. Deleting information may affect access to your account, course history or certificate verification.

12. Children and young learners

The academy is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental or guardian authorisation is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the necessary authorisation, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

13. Third-party websites

Our website may link to external websites or services. Those services operate independently and have their own privacy notices. Please review their policies before providing them with personal information.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when a material update is published. Please review this page periodically.

15. Applicable privacy laws

We aim to handle personal information transparently and securely and to comply with privacy laws that apply to our activities. Where applicable, this includes Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law No. 13 of 2016 and other relevant data protection requirements. This policy is general information, not legal advice or a certification of compliance. The law applicable to a particular learner or processing activity may depend on the circumstances.
